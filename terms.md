## 4. Test Structure

The test starts with one question. After every question the bot estimates your IQ and continues to test you until it feels confident enough to give you the score.

Questions are adapted depending on your results:

- Answer well -&gt; harder questions
- Answer badly -&gt; easier questions
- Skip -&gt; treated like a wrong answer

No test length limit. Stops when the bot feels confident enough.

## 5. Domains and Tags

You can select the domain to be tested on (Math, History, Coding, etc.). The bot tests your knowledge of the chosen domain and adds a **domain tag** to your profile, e.g. `Coding (Python): 120`.

Domain tags are displayed on your profile(bot profile), not your nickname.

## 6. Brainrot Questions

The bot may give you a brainrot question. If you answer it correctly, you get **negative marks**. It means knowing brainrot is not a badge here.

If you keep answering brainrot, the bot will keep testing you with brainrot and give you more negatives.

Knowing brainrot is a measurable skill. It is not rewarded.

## 7. Bonus Penalties

- **Say "I'm 100% sure" and get it wrong?** -15 IQ
- **Can't explain the opposing opinion?** -25 IQ. You're now "smooth".
- **Retest and get lower result?** New score replaces the old one. Yes, it can go down. Yes, it's the point.

"Smooth" is a technical term. Not an insult. (Well, kind of).
